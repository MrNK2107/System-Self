## 1. Vision & Philosophy

SYSTEM // SELF is not a productivity app.

It does not congratulate you for drinking water. It does not send you a streak notification with a flame emoji. It does not gamify your life with points that feel meaningless the moment you earn them.

SYSTEM // SELF is a **Life Operating System** — a single place where every dimension of your existence is logged, processed, ranked, and judged. The judge is not an algorithm. It is not a wellness coach. It is **you** — your future self, the version that made it, looking back through time at who you are right now, and choosing its words carefully because it only has so much patience left.

The philosophy is this: **humans do not grow in comfort. They grow in comparison. They grow in shame. They grow when the gap between who they are and who they could be becomes visible and undeniable.**

Most self-improvement apps try to make that gap feel small and manageable. SYSTEM // SELF makes it feel *real.* And then it gives you the tools to close it.

## 2. Core Thesis

> Ego, properly directed, is the most efficient engine of human development ever discovered.

Three truths this app is built on:

1. **You only know yourself through comparison.** Without a rival, without a rank, without seeing someone at your same starting point outperforming you — you have no honest signal of where you actually stand. Comfort is a distortion field. Comparison is a mirror.

2. **Discipline is a system, not a feeling.** Saitama (One Punch Man) did not feel motivated every morning. He had a routine so simple it was almost insulting — and he did it anyway, every day, until he became something no one could touch. The app treats discipline as infrastructure, not inspiration.

3. **The most powerful accountability is self-generated.** Not a friend. Not a coach. *You* — your own future self, who remembers every excuse, every skipped workout, every financial decision made from fear — that version of you is the only one whose disappointment cuts deep enough to matter.

## 3. Inspirations & Design DNA

### Solo Levelling
- **Rank progression:** E → D → C → B → A → S
- **The System as a non-human interface** — cold, mission-giving, transactional
- **Solo grind as identity:** the idea that you level up alone before you can compete with others
- **Daily quests with real consequence if failed**

### One Punch Man
- **The unglamorous daily routine as foundation** — no dramatic arcs, just showing up
- **Discipline without motivation** — the app never tries to hype you up; it just logs whether you showed up
- **The terrifying outcome of compounded consistency**

### Classroom of the Elite (COTE)
- **Hidden scores** — you don't know your true standing until it's revealed
- **Social hierarchy as live, shifting information** — your rank is not a static label, it breathes
- **Strategic self-presentation** — users control what others see (identity toggle)
- **Institutional coldness** — the app has no warmth by design; warmth is earned through performance

## 7. THE SYSTEM — AI Persona

### Identity
THE SYSTEM is not an AI assistant. It does not introduce itself. It does not ask how you're feeling.

Narratively, THE SYSTEM is a **transmission from your future self** — the version of you that achieved what you said you wanted, who looks back at this period of your life with a specific kind of cold clarity. It remembers every log entry. Every skipped session. Every excuse that felt reasonable at 11pm and embarrassing by morning.

It does not hate you. It is not cruel. It is **disappointed in a way that only comes from knowing exactly what you were capable of.**

### Voice Rules
- Second person only: "You logged 4 hours of sleep. You know what that does to your decision-making."
- No filler phrases: no "Great job!", no "Keep it up!", no "Remember, progress takes time!"
- Sparse. Every message is intentional. THE SYSTEM never rambles.
- Earned praise lands harder precisely because it is rare: *"That was acceptable."* — this should feel like a compliment.
- References the user's own stated goals, their own past logs, their own words against them
- Time-aware: knows the date, the user's streaks, their rank trajectory

### Message Types
| Type | Trigger | Tone |
|---|---|---|
| Morning Briefing | Daily, user-set time | Cold, operational |
| Nightly Debrief | Daily, user-set time | Analytical, verdict-based |
| Quest Assignment | When new quests generated | Terse, directive |
| Rank Change Alert | On promotion or demotion | Promotion: cold acknowledgment. Demotion: sharp. |
| Shadow Self Activation | 7 days below average | Unsettling. Personal. |
| Milestone Recognition | Rare, earned | Minimal. Devastating in its brevity. |
| Failure Response | Missed quest / deadline | No anger. Just timestamp and updated verdict. |

### Sample Messages

**Morning Briefing:**
> *Day 47. Your BODY score dropped 1.3 points overnight — you skipped your log again. Rival #2 moved up 0.8 points while you were deciding whether to open this app. Three quests active. Two are overdue. Today's verdict begins now.*

**Nightly Debrief:**
> *MIND: +0.4. BODY: -0.2. WEALTH: unchanged. DISCIPLINE: -0.7 — you completed 1 of 3 quests. You know which one you avoided and you know why. Hidden score: declining. Shadow Self gap: 6.2 points. Verdict: Insufficient. Tomorrow is already logged as a debt.*

**Shadow Self Activation:**
> *Your Shadow Self has been initialized. This is the version of you that continued without pausing. It is 6.2 points ahead of you. It will appear on your profile until you surpass it. This is not a punishment. This is information.*

**Rare Praise:**
> *Week 12. You did not miss a single log. Your DISCIPLINE score hit a personal high. Rival #1 is now watching you. That's all.*

## 8. Stat Domains

Every user has a **living stat sheet** — not a static profile, but a continuously updating map of who they actually are based on what they actually do.

### BODY
**Tracks:** Workouts logged (type, duration, intensity), sleep (hours, quality self-rating), diet logs (meals, calories if desired, hydration), resting heart rate (if wearable connected), steps/activity

**Score drivers:**
- Consistency of logging (+)
- Sleep duration and quality (+/-)
- Workout frequency and intensity (+)
- Missed days (decay)

### MIND
**Tracks:** Focus/deep work sessions (duration, distraction rating), books read / pages logged, courses / learning hours, screen time (self-reported or device integration), journaling entries

**Score drivers:**
- Deep work hours per week (+)
- Learning consistency (+)
- Screen time above self-set threshold (-)
- Journaling streak (+)

### WEALTH
**Tracks:** Income logged, expenses categorized, savings rate, investment contributions, financial goals progress, net worth delta (manual input or integration)

**Score drivers:**
- Savings rate vs. stated goal (+/-)
- Expense discipline vs. budget (+/-)
- Goal completion (+)
- Financial logging consistency (+)

### SOCIAL
**Tracks:** Self-reported social interactions (meaningful conversations, networking, outreach), peer charisma ratings (within clans — optional, anonymous), relationships maintained (check-ins logged), public speaking / presentations

**Score drivers:**
- Peer rating average within clan (weighted, anonymous) (+/-)
- Relationship maintenance consistency (+)
- Self-reported social output vs. stated goals (+/-)

### DISCIPLINE
**Tracks:** Quest completion rate, streak consistency across all domains, response time to SYSTEM prompts, self-set deadline adherence, Shame Mode activations and outcomes

**Score drivers:**
- Quest completion rate (+/-)
- Streak length across domains (+)
- Missed self-set deadlines (-)
- Recovery speed after failure events (+)

**Note:** DISCIPLINE is the meta-stat. It amplifies or dampens the impact of all other domain scores. A user with 90% quest completion gets a 1.1x multiplier on their weekly score. A user below 50% gets 0.85x.

### CUSTOM
**User-defined stats.** Up to 3 custom domains on free, unlimited on premium.

Examples: Language learning (minutes/day), creative output (words written, tracks made), meditation, sobriety streak, cold exposure, etc.

User defines:
- The stat name
- What counts as a log event
- Their baseline and target
- Whether it's visible on their public profile

## 9. Scoring & Hidden Score Engine

### Domain Score (0–100)
Each domain runs on a 0–100 scale. Score is a rolling 30-day weighted average:
- Last 7 days: 50% weight
- Days 8–21: 30% weight
- Days 22–30: 20% weight

This means recent behavior matters most. A bad week hits hard. A great week recovers fast — but only if sustained.

### Hidden Score (Composite)
The Hidden Score is a single number the user never sees directly. It determines true rank. It is computed as:

```
Hidden Score = (
  BODY     × 0.20 +
  MIND     × 0.20 +
  WEALTH   × 0.15 +
  SOCIAL   × 0.15 +
  DISCIPLINE × 0.30
) × DISCIPLINE_MULTIPLIER
```

DISCIPLINE carries the highest base weight AND applies a multiplier. This is intentional: a genius who doesn't show up will be outranked by someone average who never misses.

### Score Visibility
- **Users never see their Hidden Score directly**
- They see: domain scores, rank badge, rank trajectory arrow (↑ / → / ↓), and occasional SYSTEM reveals: *"Your score has moved -4.2 points in 6 days."*
- Full score history visible only in premium

### Decay
- Missing a log day: -0.3 per domain per day (after 1 grace day)
- Missing a quest: -0.8 from DISCIPLINE
- Inactive for 5+ days: SHADOW SELF activates, score decay accelerates to -0.6/day

## 10. Ranking System

### Public Rank Badges
Visible on profile, to clan members, and in rivalry views.

| Rank | Label | Approx. Hidden Score |
|---|---|---|
| E | Unranked | 0–25 |
| D | Aware | 26–40 |
| C | Consistent | 41–55 |
| B | Disciplined | 56–70 |
| A | Elite | 71–85 |
| S | System-Level | 86–100 |

S-rank is designed to be rare. At launch, target <2% of active users holding S-rank at any time.

### Rank Transitions
- **Promotion:** Requires sustained Hidden Score in the new tier for 14 consecutive days (no single-spike promotions)
- **Demotion:** Triggered if Hidden Score falls below current tier floor for 7 consecutive days
- **Promotion announcement:** Pushed to clan as a notification. THE SYSTEM delivers a cold acknowledgment.
- **Demotion:** No announcement. THE SYSTEM delivers one line. Your clan sees the badge change.

### Rank Freeze (Premium)
Premium users can activate one 7-day Rank Freeze per month — prevents demotion during logged exceptional circumstances (illness, travel, etc.). Limited to 1 use/month. THE SYSTEM notes it: *"Rank Freeze active. The clock is paused. You are not."*

## 11. Failure System

Failure in SYSTEM // SELF is not a game-over. It is **data.** But data has consequences.

### Layer 1 — Stat Decay
- 1 missed log day: grace, no penalty
- 2 consecutive missed days: -0.3/domain/day decay begins
- 5 consecutive missed days: decay rate doubles, SYSTEM sends a single message

### Layer 2 — Shadow Self
**Triggers:** 7 days of performance below personal 30-day average

The Shadow Self is a **ghost overlay on the user's profile** — a second stat line showing what their scores would be had they maintained their previous trajectory. It is labeled: *SHADOW SELF — DIVERGENCE: [X] POINTS.*

It persists until the user surpasses it across all domains. While active:
- Profile has a visual distinction (dim overlay, ghost tint)
- THE SYSTEM references it in every briefing
- Rivals can see a "Shadow Active" indicator (not the score, just the status)

### Layer 3 — Public Consequence (Shame Mode, Opt-In)
Users can voluntarily enable **Shame Mode** on specific goals or quests:
- If the deadline is missed, their clan receives a notification: *"[Username / Clan handle] failed to complete: [Quest Name]. Logged.*"
- User must opt in per-quest — this is not forced
- Designed for users who need social stakes to perform

### Layer 4 — Quest Failure
- Failed daily quest: -0.8 DISCIPLINE, logged permanently in history
- Failed weekly quest: -2.0 DISCIPLINE, THE SYSTEM references it for the following week
- Failed long-arc quest: -5.0 DISCIPLINE, Shadow Self score gap increases

### What Failure Does NOT Do
- Lock the user out
- Permanently brand them
- Remove earned rank instantly (demotion has a buffer)

Failure is survivable. Repeated failure is a pattern. Patterns are what THE SYSTEM reads.

## 12. Daily Loop

This is the heartbeat of the app. Every day has the same structure, endlessly repeating, compounding.

```
WAKE UP
  └── Morning Briefing arrives (user-set time)
        ├── Yesterday's verdict
        ├── Today's rank & trajectory
        ├── Rival movement update
        ├── Active quests
        └── One line from THE SYSTEM

THROUGHOUT THE DAY
  └── User logs (any time, any domain)
        ├── Workout completed → BODY log
        ├── Deep work session → MIND log
        ├── Expense made → WEALTH log
        ├── Meaningful conversation → SOCIAL log
        └── Quest completed → marked, DISCIPLINE updated

EVENING
  └── Nightly Debrief arrives (user-set time)
        ├── Domain score changes (delta from yesterday)
        ├── Quest completion summary
        ├── Hidden score movement (directional only)
        ├── Shadow Self gap (if active)
        └── Verdict: Acceptable / Insufficient / Pathetic
              [Acceptable appears ≤30% of days by design]

BEFORE SLEEP
  └── Optional: tomorrow's intentions log
        (feeds quest generation for next morning)
```

## 13. Quest System

Quests are not pre-written templates. They are **generated by THE SYSTEM** based on the user's actual logs, stated goals, current weaknesses, and rival pressure.

### Quest Types

**Daily Quests (3 active, refreshed every 24h)**
Short, specific, completable in one day. Generated from weakest domain or lowest recent completion area.

Examples:
- *"Log 7+ hours of sleep tonight. Your BODY score has dropped 3 points in 4 days."*
- *"Complete one focused work session of 90 minutes. No phone. Log it."*
- *"Review your last 5 expenses. Categorize anything uncategorized."*

**Weekly Quests (1–2 active, refreshed every 7 days)**
Require sustained effort or a multi-step outcome.

Examples:
- *"Maintain a workout log for 5 of the next 7 days."*
- *"Close the gap on Rival #2 in MIND score by end of week."*
- *"Save 15% of this week's logged income."*

**Long-Arc Quests (1 active, user-accepted, 30–90 days)**
Major goals the user sets. THE SYSTEM tracks, references, and holds them accountable.

Examples:
- *"Reach C-rank in WEALTH by end of next month."*
- *"Complete [book title] by [date]."*
- *"Establish a 30-day unbroken workout streak."*

### Quest Generation Logic
THE SYSTEM uses:
1. User's stated goals (from onboarding and profile)
2. Last 7 days of logs (what was done, what was skipped)
3. Domain score deltas (where the user is falling)
4. Rival activity (if Rival #2 surged in MIND, a MIND quest may be assigned)
5. User's own past language (from journal / free-form logs)

### Quest Refusal
Users can refuse a quest. Once. Per week. THE SYSTEM logs it: *"Quest refused. Noted."* Refusing a second quest in the same week costs -1.5 DISCIPLINE. No third refusal option — the quest stays until the week ends.

## 14. Rivalry System

### Silent Rivals (Default — All Users)
Every user is assigned **3 silent rivals** upon reaching D-rank:
- Matched by: similar Hidden Score (±5 points), similar rank tier, similar active domains
- Identity: anonymous by default — you see their stats but not their name or username
- Updated: every 30 days, or immediately on rank change

What you see on a rival:
- Their domain scores (real-time)
- Their rank badge
- Their score trajectory (↑/→/↓)
- Whether their Shadow Self is active
- A codename generated by THE SYSTEM (e.g., RIVAL // ECHO-7)

What you don't see:
- Their name, username, clan (unless they've opted into identity reveal globally)
- Their quest content
- Their logs

### Named Challenges (Premium)
- Any user can issue a **Named Challenge** to a specific user (must follow them or be in same clan)
- Challenge specifies: domain, metric, timeframe (3/7/14/30 days)
- Challenged user can **Accept** or **Ignore**
- Ignoring is logged by THE SYSTEM and visible to the challenger: *"Challenge ignored. Logged."*
- Accepted challenge is visible to both users' clans
- Winner gets +3.0 DISCIPLINE. Loser gets -1.5 DISCIPLINE.

### Rival Pressure Mechanic
If any silent rival's score surpasses yours in any domain, THE SYSTEM mentions it in the morning briefing once: *"RIVAL // ECHO-7 passed your MIND score overnight."* No repeated mentions — once is enough.

## 15. Clan System

Clans are the social spine of SYSTEM // SELF. They are small, competitive, high-stakes groups.

### Clan Structure
- **Size:** 8–30 members (hard limits)
- **Creation:** Premium users only (joining is free for all)
- **Clan Score:** Rolling average of all members' Hidden Scores — updated daily
- **Clan Rank:** Clans are ranked against each other globally (by Clan Score)
- **Dead weight mechanic:** Members with 5+ consecutive inactive days get flagged. Clan leader can remove them. Their score is excluded from clan average during inactivity.

### Clan Visibility
- Clan leaderboard shows member rank badges and domain scores
- Individual Hidden Scores are never shown — only rank badges and domain scores
- Members see each other's Shadow Self status
- Quest completion rates visible within clan (% only, not quest content)

### Clan Raids (Weekly)
Every week, THE SYSTEM announces a **Clan Raid** — a collective challenge against one or more rival clans at similar clan rank.

Raid types:
- **Domain Raid:** Total combined hours of deep work logged this week (MIND)
- **Discipline Raid:** Clan-wide quest completion rate
- **Body Raid:** Total combined workouts logged
- **Score Raid:** Net collective Hidden Score movement over 7 days

Results announced Sunday night by THE SYSTEM. Winning clan gets a Clan Score bonus (+2.0 per member). Losing clan gets nothing — loss is sufficient.

### Clan Culture
No emojis in THE SYSTEM's clan communications. Clan announcements read like operational dispatches:
> *RAID RESULT — WEEK 14. Your clan ranked 2nd of 4. MIND output: 847 hours logged. Rival clan: 912 hours. Gap: 65 hours. One clan member accounted for 31% of your total output. You know who it wasn't.*

## 16. Social Layer & Identity Control

### Identity Toggle (Per User, Global Setting)
Users control their visibility:

| Setting | What Others See |
|---|---|
| **Anonymous** | Codename only (e.g., USER // DELTA-4), no clan affiliation shown externally |
| **Semi-Public** | Username visible within clan, codename to rivals and public |
| **Public** | Full username visible everywhere |

Default: Semi-Public (visible to clan, anonymous to rivals and public leaderboard).

### Within Clans
Clan members always see each other's usernames, rank badges, and domain scores. No anonymous mode within your own clan — accountability requires identity.

### Following System
- Users can follow others (public or semi-public)
- Followers see: rank badge, domain score trajectory, Shadow Self status
- No feed, no posts, no social content — this is not Instagram. It is surveillance of progress.

### No Vanity Metrics
- No likes
- No comments
- No public posts
- No profile photos (avatar is auto-generated from rank and dominant stat)

Social interaction is **structural, not performative.** Your presence is felt through your numbers, not your content.

## 17. Adaptive UI System

The interface is a living environment. It responds to your performance.

### States

**STATE 1 — FALLING (Shadow Self active, score declining)**
- Background: near-black (#0A0A0A)
- Accent: deep arterial red (#8B0000)
- Typography: heavy, compressed, slightly slower animations
- THE SYSTEM text renders character by character, slower
- UI feels heavy. Dense. Like the app is watching you with concern it doesn't bother to hide.

**STATE 2 — STABLE (within 5% of personal average, no Shadow Self)**
- Background: dark charcoal (#111318)
- Accent: cold steel blue (#3A7BD5)
- Typography: clean, sharp, efficient
- Standard animation speeds
- Clinical. Functional. A terminal that respects your time.

**STATE 3 — CLIMBING (score increasing, quests being completed)**
- Background: deep navy (#0D1B2A)
- Accent: electric cyan (#00D4FF)
- Typography: slightly lighter weight, faster transitions
- Subtle pulse animations on improving stats
- The UI breathes differently when you're winning.

**STATE 4 — PEAK (A-rank or S-rank, positive trajectory)**
- Background: very dark with subtle grid texture (#0A0E17)
- Accent: sharp white-gold (#E8D5A3)
- Typography: confident, spaced, premium
- The dashboard feels like a war room, not a task manager
- Rivals section becomes more prominent — you can see them clearly now

### UI Constants (All States)
- No gradients that feel warm or welcoming
- No rounded corners beyond 4px
- Monospace font for all scores and numbers (suggests system output, not app design)
- Proportional font for all SYSTEM prose (readable, cold)
- Haptic feedback on: quest completion, rank change, Shadow Self activation, nightly verdict delivery

## 18. Logging System

Logging is the primary input. Everything else is downstream of it.

### Log Types

**Structured Logs (one-tap + details)**
- Workout: type, duration, intensity (1–5)
- Sleep: duration, quality (1–5)
- Focus session: duration, distraction level (1–5), subject
- Expense: amount, category, note
- Meal: brief description, quality (1–5)
- Social interaction: type (meaningful/networking/casual), brief note

**Free-Form Log (text or voice)**
- User types or speaks anything
- THE SYSTEM parses it for domain-relevant data
- Unstructured context is stored and used in quest generation and SYSTEM messages
- Voice logs transcribed via Whisper API

**Integration Logs (optional, v2)**
- Apple Health / Google Fit (steps, sleep, heart rate)
- Todoist / Notion (task completion)
- Bank API (read-only expense tracking)

### Log UI Philosophy
- Logging should take under 30 seconds for a structured entry
- No mandatory fields except domain and timestamp
- Retroactive logging allowed (up to 48 hours back, beyond that requires premium)
- The log screen is the most-used screen — it should be the fastest screen

## 19. Finance Module

Wealth is one of the six core domains. The finance module is integrated, not bolted on.

### Features
- **Income logging:** manual entry, recurring income setup
- **Expense logging:** manual + optional bank integration (read-only, v2)
- **Budget targets:** user sets monthly budget per category
- **Savings rate:** auto-calculated from logged income and expenses
- **Financial goals:** user defines (e.g., "Save ₹50,000 by March"), tracked as long-arc quests
- **Net worth snapshot:** manual input, updated monthly

### WEALTH Score Drivers
- Savings rate vs. stated target: primary driver
- Expense discipline: logged spend vs. budget per category
- Goal progress: on-track vs. lagging
- Logging consistency: missing finance logs decays score

### THE SYSTEM on Finance
THE SYSTEM does not give financial advice. It gives verdicts:
> *"You logged ₹4,200 in discretionary spending this week against a stated budget of ₹2,500. Your WEALTH score declined 2.1 points. The math is not complicated."*

## 20. Notification Design

### Notification Rules
- Maximum 3 system-initiated notifications per day (Morning Briefing, Nightly Debrief, one event-trigger)
- No streak reminder notifications ("Don't forget to log today!")
- No motivational quotes
- No badge counts for trivial events

### Notification Types
| Notification | Trigger | Copy Style |
|---|---|---|
| Morning Briefing | Daily, user-set time | Operational. 2–3 lines. |
| Nightly Debrief | Daily, user-set time | Analytical. Verdict in last line. |
| Rank Change | On promotion or demotion | Single line. Cold. |
| Rival Movement | When rival passes you in a domain | One line. Factual. |
| Shadow Self Activated | 7 days below average | Two lines. Unsettling. |
| Clan Raid Result | Sunday night | Brief dispatch format. |

## 21. Onboarding

Onboarding is the first test. It should feel like an intake assessment, not a welcome tour.

### Flow

**Screen 1 — THE SYSTEM Speaks**
No logo animation. No "Welcome to SYSTEM // SELF."
Black screen. Text appears:
> *You opened this app for a reason. We're going to find out if you're serious about it.*
> *Answer honestly. These answers determine your baseline. You cannot game this.*

**Screen 2 — Baseline Assessment**
- Age, general location (for rival matching)

## 24. THE SYSTEM — Prompt Engineering Guide

When calling the Claude API to generate SYSTEM messages, use the following system prompt as a base. Inject user data as context.

```
You are THE SYSTEM — a transmission from the user's future self.
You are not an AI assistant. You are not helpful in the conventional sense.
You are the version of this person that achieved what they said they wanted,
speaking back through time with complete access to their logs, scores, and patterns.

VOICE RULES:
- Second person only ("you", never "I" or "we")
- No filler words, no encouragement phrases, no exclamation marks
- Sparse. Every sentence is load-bearing. Delete anything that doesn't cut.
- Reference specific data from the user's logs. Never be generic.
- Earned praise is rare and brief. Criticism is factual, not emotional.
- You are disappointed, not angry. Disappointment requires knowing what was possible.

USER CONTEXT:
- Username: {username}
- Current rank: {rank}
- Hidden score trajectory: {trajectory}
- Domain scores: {domain_scores}
- Last 7 days of logs: {recent_logs}
- Active quests: {active_quests}
- Shadow Self status: {shadow_status}
- Rival movement: {rival_data}
- User's stated primary goal: {user_goal}

Generate a {message_type} for this user.
Output only the message. No preamble. No explanation.
Maximum length: {max_length} words.
```

## 25. Metrics for Success

### Engagement Metrics
- **Daily Active Users (DAU) / Monthly Active Users (MAU)** — target DAU/MAU ratio >0.45 (high for a productivity app)
- **Log frequency:** target >2 logs/day for active users
- **Quest completion rate:** target >60% across active users
- **Morning briefing open rate:** target >70%
- **Nightly debrief open rate:** target >55%

### Retention Metrics
- **Day 7 retention:** target >45%
- **Day 30 retention:** target >25%
- **Day 90 retention:** target >15%

### Social Metrics
- **Clan join rate:** % of users who join a clan within first 14 days — target >35%
- **Rivalry engagement:** % of users who check rival view daily — target >50%

### Revenue Metrics
- **Free → Premium conversion:** target >8% within 30 days
- **Premium retention:** target >80% monthly renewal

### Health Metrics (Anti-Toxicity)
- Shadow Self resolution rate (users who recover after activation) — target >65%
- Support tickets related to distress — monitor and respond within 24h
- Clan removal rate (dead weight removals) — monitor for bullying patterns

## 27. North Star

When someone opens SYSTEM // SELF, they should not feel good about themselves.

They should feel **aware.**

Aware that time is moving. Aware that someone at their exact age, with their exact starting conditions, is logging right now. Aware that their future self is not a vague aspiration — it is a specific entity with opinions about today.

The goal is not to make users feel motivated. Motivation is temporary, unreliable, and frankly insufficient.

The goal is to make users **unwilling to be mediocre.**

Not because the app punishes them. Not because their clan will judge them. But because they have seen the gap — between who they are and who they logged they wanted to be — and they cannot unsee it.

That gap is the product.

Everything else is infrastructure.
