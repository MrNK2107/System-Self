# Privacy Policy

Last updated: DRAFT. This template describes what SYSTEM // SELF actually stores. A lawyer must review and complete it before launch.

## 1. Who we are

SYSTEM // SELF ("we", "the app") is a self-improvement game. Questions about your data: **privacy@systemself.app** (placeholder address, to be confirmed).

## 2. Who can use the app

You must be **16 or older**. We ask for your birth year when you sign up and do not create accounts for people under 16. If you believe a child has signed up, contact us and we will delete the account.

## 3. What we collect

**Account**
- Email address, password (stored only as a one-way hash), username, birth year, timezone, the time you accepted the terms.
- Sign-in sessions (hashed refresh tokens, device user agent) and one-time email codes (hashed). These are deleted when they expire or when you sign out.

**Profile and onboarding**
- Your goal, weakness, identity setting, morning and nightly times, starting self-ratings, codenames, rank, and currency and savings target.

**What you log and do**
- Logs for the five domains (BODY, MIND, WEALTH, SOCIAL, DISCIPLINE), including any text you write.
- Quests, quest chains, completions, score events, daily score snapshots, milestones, achievements, debriefs, rank freezes, seasons and comebacks.
- Gym data: workout plans, sessions, sets, personal records, body-part scores and gym crowd reports you submit.
- Custom domains and their logs.
- Finance data you enter: budgets, recurring income, goals, net-worth snapshots and weekly finance summaries.

**Social features**
- Who you follow and block, reports you file, your clan membership and clan messages about you, duo partner, duo check-ins, rivals assigned to you, named challenges, and the peer ratings you give. Peer ratings you receive are stored per rater but you only ever see the weekly average.

**Notifications and billing**
- Push notification tokens for your devices, your notification preferences, a log of notifications sent, and notices shown in the app.
- Your subscription status and billing events from RevenueCat (event type and time, not card details).

**Usage and AI**
- Basic product analytics events (for example, app opened).
- Counts of AI features used per day, and the AI messages and quests generated for you.

We do **not** collect your contacts, precise location or advertising identifiers.

## 4. Why we use it

- To run the game: scoring, ranks, quests, social features and streaks.
- To personalise coaching messages, quests and debriefs.
- To sync your account across sessions and devices.
- To send notifications you have enabled.
- To provide and verify subscriptions, prevent abuse, secure accounts and fix bugs.
- To understand, in aggregate, which features are used.

We do not sell your data and do not show ads.

## 5. AI processing (Google Gemini)

Some features are written by AI: coaching messages, generated quests, debriefs and rival taunts. To produce them we send a limited summary of your game state (rank, scores, goal, recent quests and logs) to **Google Gemini** through Google's API.

If you use the **voice log** feature (Pro), the **audio you record is sent to Google Gemini to be transcribed**. We keep the audio in memory only for the request and **do not store it**. The resulting text is shown to you, and becomes a log only if you confirm it.

Google processes this data under its own terms for API customers. Do not say anything in a voice log or free text that you do not want processed this way.

## 6. Who else sees your data

- **Other players** see only what your identity setting allows (a codename, or your username to clanmates or publicly if you choose). They never see your hidden score, your logs, your finance data or who rated them.
- **Service providers** that process data for us: our hosting and database provider, our email sender, Google (Gemini), RevenueCat (subscriptions) and push delivery services (Expo, Apple, Google).
- **Payments** are handled entirely by the **Apple App Store and Google Play**, with entitlement management by **RevenueCat**. We never see your card or payment details.
- We may disclose data if required by law.

## 7. Retention

- Your account data is kept until you delete your account.
- **Analytics events: 90 days**, then deleted automatically.
- **Push notification log: 30 days**, then deleted automatically. Push tokens of devices with no active session for 30 days are removed.
- **Billing events: 180 days.** After you delete your account, remaining billing events are anonymised immediately (their content is wiped and they are no longer linked to you) and deleted after 180 days.
- **Account deletion is immediate and cascading**: your profile, logs, quests, finance, gym, social and every other table keyed to you are deleted at once. Other players' notices that named you then show FORMER OPERATOR. Reports other players filed about you are kept without a link to your account, for safety review.
- Backups of the database may hold deleted data for a short period before rotating out.

## 8. Your rights

- **Export**: Settings, "Export my data" gives you everything we store about you as a JSON file. It leaves out secrets and other people's data.
- **Deletion**: Settings, Account, "Delete account" deletes your account and data immediately.
- **Access, correction, objection**: contact **privacy@systemself.app**. Depending on where you live (for example under India's DPDP Act or the GDPR) you may have further rights, including to complain to your data protection authority.

## 9. Security

Passwords are hashed, traffic is encrypted in transit, and access to production data is limited. No system is perfectly secure. Tell us at privacy@systemself.app if you find a problem.

## 10. Changes

We will update this policy when the app changes and tell you in the app before material changes apply.
